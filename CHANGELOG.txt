Version: Unreleased
Date: 11.10.2026

  Changes: 
    - Updated the game engine to Bevy 0.19.
    - New save layout: one folder per world, with world.ron for the metadata, one file per chunk and one file per player. Worlds from older versions are converted on first load, the old file is kept as world.ron.legacy.
    - The world and the connected players are saved automatically every 60 seconds.
    - Block changes are now sent to the players as small updates instead of whole chunks.
    - World generation is deterministic: a seed always gives the same world.
    - Added a chat (T key).
    - Added sounds: footsteps and landings, mining, breaking and placing blocks by material, other players' blocks and steps heard from where they are, and button clicks. Their volumes are set in Settings > Audio.
    - Fixed players spawning inside stone.
    - Fixed the world not being saved when leaving the game.
    - Fixed the fox sinking into the ground; it now stays on it.
    - Animals live in the world: herds of sheep on the plains and mountains, foxes in the forests and on the ice plains. They wander, sheep graze, and foxes flee the players.
    - Added fox and sheep spawn eggs: used on a block, they bring their animal there.
    - Fixed crashes when a client sent invalid data: usernames are checked (1 to 32 characters) and the number of inputs per tick is limited.
    - Fixed the constant position corrections (rollbacks) of the player: the client now resumes its prediction from the full state sent by the server.
    - Fixed the server list being emptied when its file could not be read.
    - Fixed a world disappearing from the list although its deletion had failed.
    - Fixed players being announced at the position of the last player who joined.
    - Fixed crashes when a texture pack lacks a texture, when a port is already in use and when the connection cannot be opened: the game logs the error and goes back to the menu.
    - Fixed blocks breaking faster at higher frame rates: a block now takes the same time to break on any screen.
    - The mob draw distance can no longer go below one chunk.
    - Added a render distance, from 1 to 8 chunks, set in Settings > Video or with O and P: the server sends the chunks within it around each player and unloads the farther ones. A dedicated server caps it with --max-render-distance (6 by default).
    - Fixed every player receiving the chunks around all the other players.
    - A broken block now drops its item on the ground as a small spinning copy, which the players pick up by walking over it. Items of the same kind lying together merge, items left for 5 minutes vanish, and the items on the ground are saved with the world.
    - Added the chest, crafted from a ring of eight planks: right click opens it, and its 27 slots keep their items in the world, saved with it. A broken chest drops what it held. Sneak to place a block against a chest or a crafting table instead of opening it.
    - Added sprinting (Left Ctrl while walking forward, also in flight), sneaking (Left Shift: slower, and a sneaking player never walks off an edge) and swimming: sprinting in the water swims where the player looks, Space and Left Shift swim up and down.
    - A jump is now 1.3 blocks high, instead of 6.8.
    - Fixed holding jump under a block, which stuck the player under it.
    - Added the survival and creative game modes, set with /gamemode <creative|survival>. New players start in survival: they have 10 hearts, cannot fly, and lose one point of health per block of a fall beyond 3. Health comes back by a point every 4 seconds. A dead player sees a death screen, and Respawn takes it back to the spawn of the world.
    - Players now spawn on the ground, on the column nearest the centre of the world that no tree or cactus tops, instead of falling from y = 80.
    - Added wooden tools, crafted on a crafting table from planks and sticks like in Minecraft: the pickaxe, the axe, the shovel, the hoe and the sword. A tool breaks the blocks it is made for twice as fast (stone for the pickaxe, wood for the axe, dirt and sand for the shovel, leaves for the hoe and the sword), and wears out after 59 blocks; its slot shows a bar of what is left. Stone, cobblestone and furnaces now drop nothing unless broken with a pickaxe.
    - Added coal, iron and gold ore. Like stone, they drop something only when broken with a pickaxe: coal ore drops coal, and iron and gold ore drop themselves until they can be smelted.
    - Biomes are about three times wider, with winding borders, and the land slopes into the sea instead of stepping at the border of two biomes. Shores are sand; the seas are deeper, and their uneven floors are sand in the shallows, then gravel and stone deeper down. Worlds created before keep their explored land; new land around it uses the new generation.
    - Added mountain ranges: long chains of peaks up to about 165 blocks high, bare stone above 104 and snow-capped above 122, with steep faces left bare. The mountain biomes now come from the altitude.
    - Added rivers that wind through the lowlands in sandy beds and valleys, and lakes with gently sloping shores that deepen toward their middle, on uneven beds of sand, gravel and stone. Water in the ice plains freezes over.
    - Added caves: wide winding tunnels that run level or sloping and open at the surface through grass, sand and snow, and wide chambers deep underground. They stay clear of the water of the seas, rivers and lakes.
    - New players appear on open, dry ground near the centre of the world instead of always at its very centre.
    - Added coal, iron and gold ore. Coal ore drops coal; iron and gold ore drop themselves until they can be smelted.
    - Added gravel, found on the floors of the seas, lakes and rivers.
    - Ores generate in clusters and veins: coal up to y 128, iron up to y 64, and the rarer gold up to y 32.
    - Mods declare themselves in a mod.toml: their name, version, authors, icon and the mods they need, which load first. A mod whose dependencies are missing or off waits for them, and a mod that fails takes back the commands it added.
    - Added the Mods screen, on the main menu: every mod of the game folder with its switch, a search, and Turn all on and Turn all off. It says why a mod cannot load, and saves the mods turned off in mods.ron.
    - The properties of the blocks come from the base mod, in data/mods/rustcraft/blocks.toml: break times, tools, drops, boxes, light. A base mod that does not read stops the game at start and says why.
    - The base mod shows in the Mods screen as Rustcraft and can be turned off; no world opens without it, and the screen and the world say so.
    - Mods hand tables to the mods that need them: rustcraft.export as they load, rustcraft.import from a mod their mod.toml names.
    - Added the example mods Shapes, Watchtower, which needs it, and Beacon, which works with it.

    - Implemented multiplayer functionality.

Version: 0.7   
Date: 25.11.2024    

  Changes: 
    - Added biomes with 6 types: Plains, Forest, Medium Mountain, High Mountain, Desert, and Ice Plain. Biomes are determined using Perlin noise (temperature and humidity).
    - Added tree spawning in Forest biomes.
    - Added flowers (poppies and dandelions) in Plains and Forest biomes.
    - You can now connect to a server (multiplayer gameplay not functional yet).
    - Added textures for items and blocks using an atlas system.
    - Refactored the meshing system to support complex shapes (e.g., stairs and slabs).
    - Added vertex coloring for grass blocks.
