# Taming, breeding and riding

Every farm animal eats something you can grow or hunt. Feed it to make it breed, to make a young one grow faster, or, for wolves, cats and steeds, to tame it. A tamed horse with a saddle is the fastest way to cross land; a boat is the way across water.

## What each animal eats

Hold the food and use it on the animal (right click). Holding the button feeds again every half second. One item is used per feeding, and only when the animal takes it.

{{table animals.food | Animal | Food | Tameable | Yes | No}}

## Taming

[[mob:Wolf]], [[mob:Cat]], [[mob:Horse]], [[mob:Donkey]] and [[mob:Mule]] can be tamed. Feed a wild one its food: each feeding has a **1 in 3** chance to tame it, and the food is eaten either way. A tamed animal belongs to the player who fed it. A wild animal that was fleeing from you stops once tamed.

Your first tamed animal, or your first young one born, earns the achievement [[page:achievements|Good Company]].

### Wolves and cats

- A tamed wolf or cat follows you. It walks to you once you are more than 6 blocks away, stops at 2.5 blocks, and is brought to your side if it falls more than 24 blocks behind while you stand on the ground.
- Use a tamed wolf or cat to make it sit, and again to make it follow.
- **Wolves fight for you.** A wolf hunts what you strike within 24 blocks, and any hostile that goes at you within 12 blocks. It runs at 4.5 blocks a second, bites for 4 every second, and gives up a prey more than 24 blocks from it or from you.
- **Cats scare hostiles.** [[mob:Gnawer|Gnawers]] and [[mob:GustOwl|Gust Owls]] keep 8 blocks away from a tamed cat.

### Healing pets

A hurt tamed wolf, horse, donkey or mule eats its food to heal instead of breeding. Tamed cats are not healed this way.

| Food | Health back |
|---|---:|
| Roast meat | 6 |
| [[Carrot]] or [[Apple]] | 4 |
| Raw meat | 3 |
| Anything else it eats ([[Rye]]) | 2 |

## Breeding

1. Feed an adult its food. It wants a mate for **30 seconds**.
2. Feed a second adult of the same kind. Two animals that want a mate look for each other within 8 blocks, walk together, and a young one is born once they are 1.5 blocks apart.
3. Each parent must wait **5 minutes** before it breeds again.

- Wolves, cats, horses, donkeys and mules breed only once tamed, and both parents must belong to the same player. Their young one belongs to that player too.
- A [[mob:Horse]] and a [[mob:Donkey]] give a [[mob:Mule]]. Mules never breed.
- A young animal grows up in **10 minutes**. Each time you feed it, it grows up 1 minute sooner.
- An animal that already wants a mate, or bred not long ago, refuses its food.

## Wool, milk and eggs

- **Shearing.** Use an adult [[mob:Sheep]] with almost anything in hand (not food, a bow, a fishing rod or a spawn egg) for 1 to 2 [[Wool]]. Its wool grows back after it grazes. A sheared sheep drops no wool when killed.
- **Milking.** Use a [[Bucket]] on an adult [[mob:Cow]] for a [[MilkBucket]]. Drinking it removes every status effect.
- **Eggs.** Each adult [[mob:Chicken]] lays an [[Egg]] about every 5 minutes, by chance. Cook it into a [[FriedEgg]] in a furnace.

## Leads

{{recipe Lead}}

- Use a [[Lead]] on any land animal to lead it. It follows when more than 4 blocks away, and the lead snaps past 12 blocks, falling where the animal is.
- Use a [[Fence]] post within 5 blocks to tie the animals you lead to it. Use a lead on a tied animal to take it back in hand.
- Use an animal you lead to take the lead off: it goes back to your inventory.

## Horses

To ride, a horse, donkey or mule must be **tamed by you**, **adult** and **saddled**. Use a [[Saddle]] on it, then use it to climb on. Press **Left Shift** to get off. Unlike wolves and cats, a steed does not follow you: it waits where you left it.

{{recipe Saddle}}

Saddles also turn up in shipwreck chests, ruins and crypts, and as a fishing treasure. See [[guide:structures|structures]] and [[guide:fishing|fishing]].

### Riding

- **Gallop:** hold **Left Ctrl** to go 1.6 times faster, as long as the horse's breath lasts. Its breath comes back at half the rate it is used, and a winded horse gallops again once it has 30 % of it back.
- **Jump:** hold **Space** and let go. Held 1 second, the horse jumps its highest; a quick tap gives 30 % of that.
- A horse steps up a whole block without jumping.
- It goes slower backwards (0.4 times), sideways (0.5) and in water (0.35).
- Your fall damage counts 3 blocks less while you ride.
- A hostile's blow aimed at you is shared: your steed takes half of it, rounded up.

### Coats and stats

Every horse has a speed, a jump height and an endurance (how long it gallops). Its coat tells its speed and endurance on average: a wild horse is within 1 block a second and 2 seconds of the values below. Its jump is anywhere in the whole range, whatever the coat.

{{table animals.coats | Coat | Speed (blocks/s) | Endurance (s) | Bay | Bay | Chestnut | Chestnut | Black | Black | Grey | Grey | White | White | Dun | Dun}}

|  | Speed (blocks/s) | Jump (blocks) | Endurance (s) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Any horse | 6-12 | 1.3-3.2 | 3-16 |
| Wild donkey | 6-7 | 1.3-1.6 | 11-15 |

**Inheritance.** A foal gets the average of its parents' speed, jump and endurance, give or take 0.6 blocks a second, 0.25 blocks and 1.2 seconds. Its coat is one parent's, but 1 time in 10 it is a random coat. Breed your best horses together to raise better ones.

### Horse armour

Use horse armour on an adult horse you own to put it on; using another piece swaps them. Only horses wear it, not donkeys or mules. Each point takes 4 % off the blows the horse takes. Horse armour is crafted at a crafting table, and also found in ruins and crypts.

## Donkeys and mules with chests

Use a [[Chest]] on an adult [[mob:Donkey]] or [[mob:Mule]] you own to strap a 27-slot chest on its back. Sneak and use the animal to open it, within 5 blocks. Only its owner can open it. If the animal dies, the chest's contents drop where it fell, and so do its saddle and lead.

## Boats

{{recipe Boat}}

Use a [[Boat]] on water to put it down, then use it to get in. It has two seats: the driver at the back and a passenger at the front. It goes 8 blocks a second on water and 1 on land. Break it to get it back.
