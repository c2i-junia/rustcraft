# Your first day

A new world starts in the morning, 75 seconds after sunrise. The day lasts 10 minutes, so you
have a little under 9 minutes of light before the first night. This guide takes you from bare
hands to a lit shelter and a bedroll in that time.

The keys below are the defaults: see [[guide:controls|Controls]] to change them.

## 1. Punch a tree

Hold the left mouse button on a tree trunk to break it. Logs break by hand, and an axe only
makes it faster. Gather a few [[OakLog]]: 5 or 6 is enough to start.

While you walk, break some tall grass too. About one time in three it drops [[Fiber]], which you
will need for rope.

## 2. Planks, sticks and a crafting table

Open your inventory with E. It has a 2x2 crafting grid: one log makes 4 planks, two planks make
4 sticks, and four planks make a crafting table.

{{recipe OakPlanks}}
{{recipe Stick}}
{{recipe CraftingTable}}

Place the crafting table with the right mouse button and right-click it to open its 3x3 grid.
Every tool needs it. Any kind of planks works where the recipe says "any planks".

## 3. Wooden tools

Make a wooden pickaxe first: you need one to mine stone, and nothing else is a pickaxe at this
point. Holding your first tool earns the achievement *Better Than Bare Hands*.

{{recipe WoodenPickaxe}}
{{recipe WoodenAxe}}
{{recipe WoodenSword}}

Wooden tools wear out after 59 uses, so do not spend them on dirt. Mining stone or cobblestone
with your hand takes time and gives you nothing.

## 4. Stone tools and a furnace

Dig into a hillside or down a few blocks to reach [[Stone]]. Mined with a pickaxe, it drops
[[Cobblestone]]. Collect at least 14: 3 for a pickaxe, 2 for a sword, 8 for a furnace, and one
to spare.

{{recipe StonePickaxe}}
{{recipe StoneSword}}
{{recipe Furnace}}

Stone tools last 131 uses and work twice as fast as wooden ones. A stone pickaxe also mines iron
and tin ore, which wood cannot. Look for [[CoalOre]] in the stone: any pickaxe mines it, and the
coal makes torches.

## 5. Light

Hostile creatures only spawn in the dark: at night under the open sky, or under a roof or in a
cave at any time. No hostile spawns within 8 blocks of a light, in any direction. A torch, a
lantern, a campfire, a lit furnace, fire, lava and a few glowing blocks all count.

One coal or one charcoal and a stick make 4 torches.

{{recipe Torch}}

No coal yet? Put logs in the furnace: each log turns into [[Charcoal]], and logs or planks can
be burned as fuel to start it.

## 6. Food

Your food bar has 20 points. Health only comes back on its own while food is 18 or more, and you
cannot sprint at 6 or less. At 0 you take 1 damage every 4 seconds, and starving can kill you.

Easy food on the first day comes from animals: a [[mob:Pig]] drops 1 to 3 raw pork, a
[[mob:Cow]] 1 to 3 raw beef, a [[mob:Sheep]] 1 or 2 raw mutton, a [[mob:Chicken]] 1 raw chicken.
Raw meat restores little; cooked, it is worth far more:

| Food | Raw | Cooked |
|---|---:|---:|
| [[RawPork]] or [[RawBeef]] | 3 | 8 |
| [[RawMutton]] | 2 | 6 |
| [[RawChicken]] | 2 | 6 |

Cook in the furnace (10 seconds per item), or on a campfire. A lit campfire cooks a piece of raw
food at once when you right-click it while holding the food. It also gives light.

{{recipe Campfire}}

Any kind of log works for the campfire, as long as all three are the same. [[Apple]] and
[[Berries]] are food you can eat as they are. See [[page:food|Food]] and
[[guide:health|Health and hunger]] for more.

## 7. A shelter

Any closed room works: walls of planks, cobblestone or dirt, a roof, and a torch inside so that
nothing spawns there. Dig into a hill if you are short of time. A door needs six oak planks, and
a chest keeps what you do not want to carry.

{{recipe Door}}
{{recipe Chest}}

[[mob:Stumpling]]s, which spawn on grass at night, open doors. Light the ground around your door
too, or close the entrance with a block.

## 8. A bedroll

A bedroll needs 3 wool and 2 rope. Wool comes from sheep: kill one for 1 wool, or right-click an
adult sheep with almost any item to shear it for 1 or 2, and its wool grows back. Three fiber
make 2 rope.

{{recipe Rope}}
{{recipe WhiteBedroll}}

Place the bedroll and right-click it. That sets your respawn point there ("You will come back
here") and earns *Somewhere to Come Back To*. If the bedroll is broken, you come back at the
world spawn instead. Wool of other colours makes bedrolls of those colours.

At night, right-clicking it also puts you to sleep. When every player online is asleep, the
night is skipped and the sun rises.

## 9. The first night

The night lasts 10 minutes. Hostiles spawn 24 to 64 blocks away from you, so they come to you
from the dark:

- [[mob:Stumpling]] on grass at night. It sinks into the ground at dawn.
- [[mob:Bramble]] in the woods at night. It never moves, but poisons you if you come close.
- [[mob:Gnawer]] in caves below height 50, in packs of 2 to 4.

You have three ways through it:

- **Sleep** on your bedroll. It is the safest, but a night slept away does not count for the
  achievement *Still Here at Dawn*.
- **Stay awake** in your lit shelter and do something useful: smelt, craft, or dig a mine from
  inside. Being alive from nightfall to dawn earns *Still Here at Dawn*.
- **Fight** with your stone sword. Your first kill earns *Holding Your Ground*. See
  [[guide:combat|Combat]].

If you die, you drop everything you carry where you fell, and your death point is saved on your
map. Items on the ground disappear after 5 minutes, so go back quickly.
