# Progression

Rustcraft has no levels to grind: you get stronger by making better tools and armour, and the
better materials lie deeper. The story ends at the bottom of the world, in the lair of the
[[mob:EmberMother]]. This page is the map of that road; each step links to the guide that covers
it.

## Tool tiers

Every tool comes in each material. A pickaxe can only mine an ore if its tier is high enough:
mined with a lower one, the block breaks and drops nothing. Speed is how many times faster than
your bare hand it breaks the blocks it is made for.

{{table progression.tools | Tier | Made of | Pickaxe mines | Speed | Uses | Sword damage | Stone, coal, copper | + tin, iron | + gold, diamond | + obsidian | Any planks | Any cobblestone}}

Gold is the odd one: the fastest tool of all, but it wears out after 32 uses and mines only what
wood mines. Bronze is a step between stone and iron: it mines the same ores as stone, but lasts
longer and digs faster. Each pickaxe also mines everything the tiers under it mine.

See [[page:tools|Tools]] and [[page:weapons|Weapons]] for every item, and [[guide:ores|Ores]] for
where each ore lies.

## Armour tiers

Each point of armour takes 4 % off the blows of creatures, arrows, fire and lava. Falls,
drowning, hunger and poison go through it. Every piece wears by 1 each time it softens a blow.

{{table progression.armour | Material | Helmet | Chestplate | Leggings | Boots | Full suit | Blows absorbed | Leather | Bronze | Iron | Diamond | {points} pt, {durability} | {points} pts, {durability}}}

Each cell gives the armour points and the durability of the piece. [[Leather]] comes from cows.
There is no gold armour. See [[page:armour|Armour]].

## The road, step by step

### 1. Wood and stone

Your first day: logs, a crafting table, a wooden pickaxe, then stone tools and a [[Furnace]]. A
furnace smelts one item every 10 seconds and is the start of every metal. Follow
[[guide:first-day|Your first day]].

### 2. Bronze

[[CopperOre]] is common from height 96 down, and any pickaxe mines it. [[TinOre]] needs a stone
pickaxe. Smelt both, then mix 3 copper ingots and 1 tin ingot into 4 bronze ingots, in the
inventory grid.

{{recipe BronzeIngot}}

Bronze gives the first metal tools and armour.

### 3. Iron

[[IronOre]] needs a stone or bronze pickaxe and lies from height 72 down to -24. Iron opens up
the bucket, the shield, the lantern and the [[Anvil]], and an iron pickaxe mines gold and
diamond.

### 4. Into the depths

The underground is split into three tiers. Each one deeper makes hostiles stronger: more health,
harder blows, more of them, and better loot. The first time you reach a tier, its name shows on
the screen.

| Tier | Below height | Hostile strength | Found there |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| The Caves | 50 | 100 % | Coal, copper, tin, iron; crypts |
| The Depths | 0 | 150 % | Gloomrock, gold, the deep cave biomes, Cinderlings |
| The Furnace | -40 | 200 % | Diamond, lava lakes from -54, sanctuaries, the lair |

A tier only counts with rock over your head: a deep sea floor is not underground. In a world
made before the deep underground existed, the Depths start below 20 and the Furnace below 12.
See [[guide:depths|The depths]].

### 5. Gold and diamond

[[GoldOre]] lies from -8 down to -60, most of it below -32. [[DiamondOre]] lies below 16 but is
mostly found below -42, in the Furnace tier. Both need an iron pickaxe. Diamond makes the best
tools and armour, and with gold, the depths compass.

### 6. Enchanting

The engraving table needs a diamond and [[Obsidian]], which only a diamond pickaxe mines.
Obsidian forms where water meets a lava source.

{{recipe EngravingTable}}

Enchantments cost experience levels: 3 for level I, 5 more for II, 8 more for III. See
[[page:enchanting|Enchanting]].

### 7. Alchemy

The alembic brews potions: healing, night vision, water breathing, fire resistance, swiftness,
strength, regeneration, and splash potions to throw. Fire resistance is the one to bring to the
Furnace tier.

{{recipe Alembic}}

See [[page:alchemy|Alchemy]].

### 8. The depths compass

Four gold ingots around a diamond. It points to the lair of the Ember Mother, the one lair of
the world, and spins once you stand at its centre.

{{recipe DepthsCompass}}

### 9. The lair

The lair is a great cavern at the lava level, with the Ember Mother asleep on an island in a
lava lake. She wakes when you enter, has 200 health (80 more for each extra player), throws
embers, summons [[mob:Cinderling]]s, and charges once she is down to half. Beaten, she drops the
Ember Heart and turns the lair's lava to obsidian. Prepare with diamond gear and fire
resistance: see [[guide:ember-mother|The Ember Mother]].

## Along the way

- [[guide:structures|Structures]]: villages, ruins, crypts and sanctuaries hold metal, tools and
  diamonds.
- [[guide:trading|Trading]]: villagers sell iron tools and armour for [[Coin]]s.
- [[guide:farming|Farming]] and [[guide:animals|Animals]]: steady food, leather and wool.
- [[guide:combat|Combat]]: bows, shields, critical hits.

## Achievements

The 21 achievements follow the same road, and the game lists them in this order. They are earned
in Survival only; open them from the game menu (Esc), under Progress. See
[[page:achievements|Achievements]].

{{table progression.achievements | **{title}**: {goal}}}

A night slept away does not count for *Still Here at Dawn*: you must be alive from the start of
the night to dawn. *The Last Ember* goes to every player in the lair when the Ember Mother
falls.
