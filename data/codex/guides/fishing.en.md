# Fishing

Cast a [[FishingRod]] into water with the use button, wait for a bite, and draw the line in while the float is under. A catch is mostly fish, sometimes junk, and now and then a treasure worth the wait.

## How to fish

1. Hold the rod and use it toward water: the float flies out at 11 blocks a second.
2. Wait. A fish bites after **5 to 25 seconds**: the float dips under the surface.
3. Use the rod again **during the bite**, which lasts about a second. Too early or too late, the line comes back empty.

The line breaks off by itself past 32 blocks. Each catch wears the rod by 1, and pulling the float out of the ground by 2. Every catch also gives 1 to 3 points of experience.

## Tips

- The [[RawGlowfin]] bites only at night. It is the ingredient of the potion of night vision, at the [[page:alchemy|alembic]].
- Shoals of [[mob:Glintfin]] and [[mob:Shoalback]] swim in the seas: beat them for fish without a rod.
- Raw fish restores little: grill it in a [[page:smelting|furnace]] first.
