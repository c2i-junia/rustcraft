---- SOUNDS AND MUSIC ----
All the sounds and the music are original, synthesised by our own scripts (MIT, like the code):
scripts/generate_sounds.py (the sound effects, the creatures' voices included),
scripts/generate_feature_sounds.py (the armour, the bow and arrows, the experience, the engraving
table, the trade and the villagers' voices, the breeding and taming, and the stings of the
progress) and
scripts/generate_music.py (the eight pieces of music in data/music/).
